# Screenshots

Captures from the live build of Coral Bay Heat — Harbor Nights
(https://ngadukun.github.io/coral-bay-heat/game/), staged scenes rendered
by the game itself. Every frame shows the Harbor Nights HUD chip
"Neon buoys 0/12" — the collectible run added in the Harbor Nights update.

| File | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| 1-cover.jpg | Cover pose: the hero car under the Coral Bay palms at dusk |
| 2-drive.jpg | Free-drive scene on the bay boulevard |
| 3-chase.jpg | Police chase scene, wanted level rising |
| 4-mission.jpg | Mission scene at night: Tito Vidal dialogue |

The Harbor Nights update adds the 3-mission Valen Cruz story pack
("Harbor Nights"), 12 neon buoy collectibles around the marina and
boardwalk, and nightly fireworks over the bay at nightfall.
